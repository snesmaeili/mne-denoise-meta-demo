# Data attribution

The demo-data archive contains derived artefacts, plus two short excerpts of
real recordings. This notice travels inside the archive so the attribution
obligations follow the data.

## Excerpts of recorded data

**`zapline_demo_raw.fif`** — 60 s of one EEG recording from:

> Liebherr, M., Corcoran, A. W., Alday, P. M., Coussens, S., Bellan, V.,
> Howlett, C. A., Immink, M. A., Kohler, M., Schlesewsky, M., &
> Bornkessel-Schlesewsky, I. (2021). EEG and behavioral correlates of
> attentional processing while walking and navigating naturalistic
> environments. *Scientific Reports*, 11, 22325.
> https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-01772-8

OpenNeuro **ds003620**, `doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds003620.v1.1.1`.

> **Licence note.** The dataset's `dataset_description.json` states `CC0`, while
> its `README` states the ODC Open Database License (ODbL). We follow the more
> restrictive reading and redistribute under share-alike terms with attribution.

**`dss_demo-epo.fif`** — 160 epochs from one participant of:

> Kappenman, E. S., Farrens, J. L., Zhang, W., Stewart, A. X., & Luck, S. J.
> (2021). ERP CORE: An open resource for human event-related potential
> research. *NeuroImage*, 225, 117465.
> https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2020.117465

ERP CORE N170, OpenNeuro `ds003067`.

> **Licence note.** The dataset's `dataset_description.json` states `CC0`, while
> its `LICENSE` file states CC BY-SA 4.0. We follow the more restrictive
> reading and redistribute under share-alike terms with attribution.

## Derived statistics only (no recorded data redistributed)

**`movement_metrics.json`, `movement_traces.npz`** — summary metrics and power
spectra computed from:

> Studnicki, A., & Ferris, D. P. Real World Table Tennis.
> OpenNeuro **ds004505**, `doi:10.18112/openneuro.ds004505.v1.0.4`. Licence: CC0.

**`asr_fixture.npz`** — entirely synthetic; generated by `_asr_fixture.py` with a
fixed seed. No recorded data.

All remaining files are metrics, spectra and provenance manifests derived from
the sources above.
